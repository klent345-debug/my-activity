Do you have any questions?

“Do you have any questions for us?” appears at first look to be a pretty innocuous question, but the opportunity to completely knock your interview out of the park or to destroy your prospects in this interview is held in the balance depending on your answer! You may think I’m being a little dramatic, but this is often the decider and anyone who doesn’t take it seriously runs the risk of being pipped to the post yet again!

Are you getting it exactly?

For centuries, humans have communicated across space and time by writing to each other – communicating concepts and history that would have never been possible face-to-face. Can you imagine Shakespeare verbally transferring MacBeth to a new generation? …Nightmare. Writing allows our thoughts and ideas to reach a much wider audience than a single conversation.

Is it clearly explained?

Another occasion was a more junior interviewee who at the end of the interview that had gone really well decided that when they were asked did they have any questions that they wouldn’t take up any more of the interviewer’s time and simply said they didn’t. The interviewer took this as a lack of interest in the role and the role was offered to a more enthusiastic candidate, in their view. It’s even more prevalent in a more junior interview where there really are a lot more options for companies so the competition is fiercer and often it can be something small that is the deciding factor on who to hire.

Course is deserving any?

How I would suggest you answer it if the interviewer has already taken you through your first 6 months and told you what they would see as your natural next step with them, and you really don’t have a question, is to state that you don’t have any questions as you really appreciate how thorough the interview process has been and they have given you all the information you need, followed by: “I would just like to reiterate my interest in working for (Company name) and in particular, you. It is a position I can see myself thriving in, and I know if I work really hard the rewards will come. So, thank you for your time in this process.”
